# 7-3-Project-Two
DriverPass project entailed creation of a scheduling and training management system for a customer who aimed at modernizing the process of preparation of students to take DMV driving test. In other words, the customer, namely DriverPass, required the system which would be capable of managing user account, scheduling driving classes, tracking progress of students, and providing access to instructional material. This particular project entailed collection of business requirements from the customer and implementation of the design of such a system based on these business requirements. Moreover, the system had to accommodate several roles of users such as students, instructors and administrators; besides, it should guarantee secure access and proper handling of data.

One of the areas where I have shown effective work is the structuring and presentation of the business requirements document. In this document, I have structured the requirements in such a way that they can be easily understood by all, and there will be no problem in tracing those requirements in terms of client's need. Also, all the requirements have been presented in an action-oriented way which is related to the functionality of the system. There has been a lot of attention towards details in the documentation of user roles and their system interactions. System design documentation also represents the same kind of consistency in modeling of the system with the help of diagrams.

Should there be a chance for me to improve any part of my work, I would develop a more detailed description of preconditions and postconditions in the use cases. Such elaboration of the descriptions would make the logic of each use case clearer and prevent misunderstanding for any developer who might have to continue working on the system in the future. In addition, I would add alternative paths to increase the number of edge cases considered in the design of the system. All of these actions would improve the completeness of the description of the system and facilitate the process of its implementation.

From the user's needs, I derived the functional requirements by analyzing the client's statements, deriving functional requirements, and formulating them as structured requirements. I analyzed the driving instruction process, which was aimed at overcoming certain difficulties that were encountered by DriverPass. This formed the basis for the development of features like scheduling, progress monitoring, and user management in the system. Taking user needs into account is important because systems that do not cater to the actual needs of the users end up being very inefficient and sometimes impossible to use. When designing a system, it is important to consider user needs to ensure the functionality of the system without any unnecessary complications.

The software design methodology that I apply is one of structured analysis, refinement and documentation. The first step involves collecting requirements from users and stakeholders as well as validating them for correctness. Then, the next step is to model the system using various models such as the use case diagrams, activity diagrams and class diagrams. These models assist in the identification of weaknesses and areas of improvement before system development starts. Future designs of systems will involve the use of structured analysis methodologies and also user-centric design approaches.
